Privacy Policy

Bhagavad Gita app by Novoverse · Effective 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bhagavad Gita app ("the app") handles on Android and iOS. The app is made by Novoverse ("we", "us"). In short: the app itself collects no personal information. Its only network use is to show ads through Google AdMob.

What the app stores on your device

All verses, translations and commentaries are bundled inside the app, so it works fully offline. The app keeps the following only on your device:

  • your bookmarks and your last-read verse,
  • your recent searches,
  • your reading settings (text size, theme, content language and which sections to show).

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads never appear inside the scripture text. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version,
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Google handles this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive this data in a form that identifies you, and we don't sell personal information.

Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent to personalized ads. If you decline, you'll see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Reading settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA isn't shared. You can change this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads).

Reading never depends on any of these choices.

Children

The app is a general-audience reading app and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The app doesn't transmit your bookmarks, searches or settings anywhere. Ad traffic is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
